A familiar face, running a modern payments desk.

Syed Ali Raza runs Karsal's digital payments counter — the place villagers come to send money, pay a bill, or settle a loan installment without a trip into the city. It's built on the same trust as any local shop: your name, your account, your money, handled by someone who lives here too.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to bring for a transaction? +

Bring your original CNIC and, if applicable, the bill or account details for the payment you need made. For larger transfers, having the recipient's registered mobile number or account number ready speeds things up.

Can I use this if I don't have an Easypaisa or JazzCash account? +

Yes. Cash transactions can be processed on your behalf even without a personal wallet account, as long as valid ID is provided — the same way an over-the-counter agent transaction works anywhere in Pakistan.

Is there a limit on how much I can send or pay? +

Transaction limits follow the same rules set by Easypaisa, JazzCash, and individual banks. Ask at the counter for the current limit on the specific service you need — this can change from time to time.

How do loan services work here? +

This is an advisory and processing service — checking your eligibility for available digital lending products, helping with the application, and processing disbursement or repayment. Terms and approval always come from the lending provider itself, not the counter.

Do you visit homes, or only serve the counter? +

Both. If you're unable to come in — due to distance, health, or timing — call or message ahead to arrange a home-to-home visit. The same services and receipts apply, just delivered at your door.

What are your hours? +

The counter is open daily for walk-in service, with home visits available on request. For urgent transactions outside normal hours, call or message ahead on the number below.
